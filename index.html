Questions? Answers.

Everything you need to know about tracking meals, building healthier habits, and using SnapFit.

What is SnapFit?

SnapFit is an AI-powered nutrition and calorie tracking app that helps users understand their meals, track calories, monitor progress, and build healthier eating habits with a simple mobile experience.

How does SnapFit estimate calories?

SnapFit uses AI-assisted food recognition and nutrition estimation to analyze meals from images, scans, or manual input. Results are estimates, so users can adjust details when needed for better accuracy.

Can I scan food with my camera?

Yes. SnapFit supports food scanning so users can quickly log meals without typing everything manually.

Where is SnapFit available?

SnapFit is available for Android and is actively being improved. An iOS version is being prepared for the App Store.

Is SnapFit free to use?

Yes. Core features are free. SnapFit Pro adds more advanced health, nutrition, tracking, and productivity tools for users who want a more complete experience.

Is SnapFit accurate?

SnapFit is designed to provide helpful calorie and nutrition estimates. Results may vary depending on the food image, portion size, ingredients, and user input.

Can SnapFit help me lose weight?

SnapFit can support weight-loss goals by helping users track calories, understand eating patterns, and stay aware of daily nutrition. It is not a medical app and should not replace professional health advice.

Does SnapFit store my food data?

SnapFit may store user data needed for features such as progress tracking, meal history, and personalized summaries. User privacy and data protection are important parts of the app’s development.

Is SnapFit a medical or dietitian app?

No. SnapFit is a wellness and nutrition tracking tool. It provides general information and estimates, but it does not replace medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment from qualified professionals.

How can I contact the SnapFit team?

You can contact the SnapFit team through snapfit-app.online or by using the contact link in the website footer.
